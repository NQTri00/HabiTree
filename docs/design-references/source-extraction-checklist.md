## Asset provenance and validation

For every extracted asset, record:
1. Exact source sheet file or conversation attachment and source region.
2. Which pixels were retained, whether background removal was needed, and whether text was removed from a reusable blank material.
3. Workspace resource path and consumers.
4. A preview over light/dark checker material to expose background remnants or clipped edges.
5. Comparison with the source at the rendered size: silhouette, proportions, material texture, palette, lighting and trim.

Do not extract an entire UI screenshot as a screen background. Preserve real text, state and actions. Do not invent a new specimen and call it source-derived. Existing nonreference domain items remain an explicit design gap until their treatment is decided; do not silently change their identity or economy.

## Screen acceptance

Review all six app routes inside the actual app shell, plus Add Task, Seed Detail and Planting dialogs. Check normal/large text and populated/empty states. Confirm Back, navigation, buying, planting, task completion/deletion/reward and tree care/decorations still dispatch their existing intents.

The 90 percent target applies to each screen/dialog, not an average. Assess composition, illustration, materials/colors, typography and controls against the corresponding source. Record remaining mismatches. Subjective visual judgment must not be presented as a measured numerical similarity score.
